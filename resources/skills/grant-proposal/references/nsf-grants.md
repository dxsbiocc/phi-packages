# NSF Grant Proposal Branch (Standard Research / CAREER)

Load this reference ONLY for U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF) proposals.

## 1. Mandatory Dual Review Criteria (Equal Weight)

| Criterion | What NSF Panels Score | Fatal Mistake to Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Intellectual Merit** | Potential to advance fundamental knowledge, creativity/originality, well-reasoned theoretical/experimental plan, PI qualification | Framing a purely clinical/disease-treatment project (NSF funds **fundamental science & engineering**, NOT clinical trials or disease-specific therapy — route clinical therapy to NIH!) |
| **Broader Impacts** | Societal benefit, STEM education/workforce training, broadening participation of underrepresented groups, open-source software/data infrastructure, public outreach | Treating Broader Impacts as a generic 3-sentence afterthought ("we will publish papers and mentor a PhD student") |

## 2. Structure & Page Budget (15-Page Project Description + 1-Page Project Summary)

1. **Project Summary (1 page strictly)**: Must contain three explicitly labeled headings: **Overview**, **Intellectual Merit**, and **Broader Impacts**.
2. **Project Description (`<= 15` pages)**:
   - Motivation & fundamental scientific/engineering gap.
   - Research Objectives & Methodology (clear milestones, validation, and risk mitigation).
   - **Results from Prior NSF Support** (mandatory if PI had NSF funding in the past 5 years; up to 5 pages max, usually 0.5–1 page).
   - **Broader Impacts Plan** (concrete activities, measurable metrics, institutional partners, timeline; for **CAREER**, must tightly integrate research + education into a 5-year career trajectory).
3. **Data Management & Sharing Plan (2 pages)**: FAIR metadata, open code/data repositories, retention schedule.

## 3. NSF Panel Red Flags

- Disease-centric / clinical-endpoint framing (triggers immediate "better suited for NIH" triage).
- Unmeasurable Broader Impacts lacking target audience, activities, or assessment plan.
- Missing `Results from Prior NSF Support` when applicable.
