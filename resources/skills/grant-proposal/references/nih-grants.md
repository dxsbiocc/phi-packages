# NIH Grant Proposal Branch (R01 / R21 / K99-R00)

Load this reference ONLY for NIH grant mechanisms.

## 1. NIH Mechanism & Page-Budget Matrix

| Mechanism | Research Strategy Limit | Preliminary Data Expectation | Core Focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **R01** | **12 pages** (+ 1-page Specific Aims) | **Strong** — must establish feasibility and support central hypothesis | Mature, hypothesis-driven 4–5 yr program |
| **R21** | **6 pages** (+ 1-page Specific Aims) | **Not required** (exploratory/developmental; concise proof-of-concept helps) | High-risk / high-reward exploratory tool or concept |
| **K99/R00** | **12 pages** (Candidate + Career Dev + Research Strategy combined) | Modest pilot data + clear separation from mentor's R01 | Training plan + mentored-to-independent transition |

## 2. NIH Review Criteria (Simplified Framework vs K-Awards)

- **Research Project Grants (`R01`, `R21`, etc. — Simplified Review Framework, Effective 2025+)**:
  - **Factor 1: Importance of the Research** (Scored 1–9) — Significance + Innovation.
  - **Factor 2: Rigor and Feasibility** (Scored 1–9) — Approach, statistical power, **Rigor of Prior Research**, **Sex as a Biological Variable (SABV)**, Pitfalls & Alternative Strategies.
  - **Factor 3: Expertise and Resources** (Unscored individually: `Appropriate` / `Additional Expertise/Resources Needed`) — Investigator track record + institutional environment.
- **Career Development Awards (`K99/R00`, `K01`, `K08`, `K23` — Excluded from Simplified Framework)**: Scored on the **5 K-award criteria**: *Candidate*, *Career Goals & Objectives*, *Career Development/Training Plan*, *Research Plan*, and *Mentors/Environment*.
- **Mandatory Separate Attachments**: Include the **2-page Data Management and Sharing (DMS) Plan** and **Authentication of Key Biological/Chemical Resources** (cell lines, antibodies, reagents).

## 3. 1-Page Specific Aims Blueprint (The Most Important Page)

1. **Opening Paragraph (~1/4 page)**: Public-health / biological burden -> critical knowledge gap -> long-term goal -> **overall objective** + **central hypothesis** (grounded in preliminary data) + **rationale**.
2. **Aims Block (~1/2 page)**: 2–3 crisp, **non-dependent** Specific Aims (`Aim 1: Determine whether...`, `Aim 2: Define the mechanism by which...`, `Aim 3: Evaluate whether...`), each with working hypothesis + brief approach (`<= 4–5` lines each).
3. **Payoff Paragraph (~1/4 page)**: Expected outcomes + vertical positive impact on the field (what becomes possible once these Aims succeed).

## 4. Top NIH Study-Section Killers

- Aim 2/3 completely depends on Aim 1 succeeding ("domino aims").
- Missing **Pitfalls & Alternative Strategies** subsection at the end of every Aim.
- Missing **Rigor of Prior Research** or ignoring **SABV** (single-sex design without justification).
- Overambitious Aim 3 crammed with 5 unrelated assays and no power justification.
