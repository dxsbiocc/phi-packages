# ERC Grant Proposal Branch (Starting / Consolidator / Advanced)

Load this reference ONLY for European Research Council (ERC) frontier research grants.

## 1. ERC Tier & Sole Criterion Matrix

- **Sole Evaluation Criterion**: **Scientific Excellence** (of the **Research Project** + of the **Principal Investigator**). No societal-impact quota or consortium requirement—ERC funds **individual-PI, high-risk / high-gain frontier research**.

| Tier | Eligibility (Years Post-PhD) | PI Excellence Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Starting Grant (StG)** | 2–7 years | Proven potential for independence; at least 1 strong publication as main author without PhD supervisor |
| **Consolidator Grant (CoG)** | 7–12 years | Consolidating own independent research team/program; strong independent track record |
| **Advanced Grant (AdG)** | Established leaders (last 10 yrs) | Major field-shaping breakthroughs and sustained international leadership |

## 2. Two-Stage B1 vs B2 Architecture (ERC WP 2026+)

| Document | Limit (WP 2026+) | Audience & Role | Writing Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Part I (in B1) — Extended Synopsis** | **5 pages** (+ narrative CV & Track Record) | **Step 1 Panel (broad generalists)** — decides if you advance to Step 2! | Crystal-clear big-picture frontier vision, why *now*, why *high-risk / high-gain*, objectives, and state of the art. **Never assume Step 1 reviewers read Part II (B2).** |
| **Part II (B2) — Full Scientific Proposal** | **7 pages** for StG/CoG/AdG (`10 pages` for Synergy; pre-2026 calls were `14 pages`) | **Step 2 Remote Specialist Referees + Panel** | Full methodology, **feasibility**, granular Work Packages (WPs), Go/No-Go milestones, risk mitigation, and resource justification. |

*(Note: Under ERC WP 2026+, formal **feasibility assessment** occurs at Step 2 / Part II, while Step 1 evaluates ground-breaking ambition and PI excellence.)*

## 3. Top ERC Panel Killers

- **Incremental "safe" science**: If a project looks like a routine next step of your last paper, ERC panels reject it as lacking *ground-breaking / high-risk, high-gain* ambition.
- **High-risk WITHOUT risk management**: Ambition must be paired with concrete **Go/No-Go milestones** and fallback routes per Work Package.
- **B1 written as a shrunken B2**: B1 must stand alone for generalist panelists; jargon-heavy B1s fail at Step 1 before specialists ever see B2.
