Purpose: make the integration verdict visual. Principle: the same cells embedded
from uncorrected and corrected PCs show whether the batch axis was removed;
one-donor-per-corner before and mixed samples within clusters after is the
success pattern. Interpretation: check that no known population disappeared —
if a whole biological group vanishes after correction, the method over-
corrected and you go back. Pitfalls: mixing alone is not success; marker
retention and known biology are the other half of the judgement.
