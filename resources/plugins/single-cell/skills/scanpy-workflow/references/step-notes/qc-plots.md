Purpose: see the distributions the thresholds will cut. Principle: the violin
shape shows the empty-droplet shoulder and the doublet tail; the highest-
expression gene list exposes ambient/ribosomal domination. Interpretation: with
several samples, plot per sample — one pooled violin hides a bad library.
Pitfalls: choose thresholds from these plots plus the quantiles, then re-read
the after-plots; a threshold that deletes a whole shoulder of real cells is a
decision to revisit, not a result.
