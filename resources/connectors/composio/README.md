Composio Connect provides a managed integration layer for AI agents, routing authenticated tool calls to Gmail, Slack, GitHub, Notion, Google Drive, Linear, HubSpot, and more than 1,000 other SaaS applications through a single MCP endpoint.

### Capabilities

- Discover available integrations and actions dynamically based on the task at hand.
- Authenticate individual third-party accounts on demand with scoped OAuth or API credentials managed by Composio.
- Chain multi-app workflows in one conversation, such as reading emails, updating spreadsheets, opening GitHub issues, and posting Slack summaries.
- View and revoke connected accounts and action permissions from a central dashboard.

### Use Cases

- Route literature alerts, collaborator emails, and Slack notifications into structured reading lists or action items.
- Sync experiment summaries, tabular results, and meeting notes to Google Drive, Google Sheets, or Notion workspaces.
- Coordinate analysis pipeline issues, pull requests, and manuscript revision checklists across GitHub and Linear.
- Aggregate form responses into shared trackers and notify team members when records require review.

### Usage Notes

- Sign in to Composio via OAuth on first connection; individual downstream apps prompt for authorization the first time you invoke them.
- Tool call quotas, supported app actions, and plan limits depend on your Composio account tier.
- Homepage: [Composio Connect](https://docs.composio.dev/docs/composio-connect). Review the permissions granted to each connected third-party app and follow its terms of service.
