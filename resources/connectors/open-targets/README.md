Open Targets Platform integrates human genetics, somatic mutations, transcriptomics, pathways, animal models, literature mining, and clinical trial records to score and contextualize target-disease associations for drug discovery.

### Capabilities

- Search targets by gene symbol, protein name, or Ensembl ID to inspect functional annotations, tractability assessments, safety liabilities, and known modulators.
- Search diseases and phenotypes by name or EFO/MONDO identifier and browse ranked candidate targets.
- Break down target-disease association scores by evidence source, separating human genetic support from expression, somatic, pathway, or text-mining signals.
- Retrieve investigational and approved drugs, mechanisms of action, clinical trial phases, and indications.
- Compare evidence profiles across multiple candidate genes to prioritize targets for functional validation.

### Research & Workflow Use Cases

- Prioritize drug targets backed by human germline genetic evidence (GWAS fine-mapping, rare coding variants, QTL colocalization) before launching wet-lab screens.
- Identify drug repurposing opportunities by cross-referencing clinical-stage compounds, mechanisms of action, and genetically supported indications.
- Annotate hit lists from CRISPR screens, differential expression, or proteomics studies with disease associations and druggability metrics.
- Audit multi-source target-disease evidence and primary citations during target review or manuscript preparation.

### Usage Notes

- No sign-in or API key is required to query the official Open Targets Platform MCP server.
- Association scores and clinical trial records update with each Open Targets release; record the platform version and query date when reporting results.
- Homepage: [Open Targets Platform MCP](https://github.com/opentargets/platform-mcp). See [Open Targets](https://platform.opentargets.org/) for licensing, data provenance, and citation guidelines.
